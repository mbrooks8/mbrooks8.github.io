Settle in, designer

You're the new interior designer in town, and everyone wants a room that feels like home. Here's what's waiting for you.

💬

Design for real characters

Every client has a style they adore, colours that make them smile and a few things they simply can't stand. Ask your questions wisely, take notes, then bring their dream room to life.

🛋️

Hundreds of cozy pieces

Bedrooms, kitchens, living rooms and more. Mix classic, modern, scandi, rustic, vintage and posh pieces, recolour anything you like, and pick the perfect floors and walls.

🍂

Seasons and celebrations

The town changes with the seasons, and festivals pop up all through the year with special stalls and furniture you can only find while the fun is on.

🌏

Furniture from around the world

Collect sets inspired by homes and cultures all over the globe, plus themes like coastal, cottagecore, japandi and boho to make every room one of a kind.

⭐

Grow your little studio

Earn fees and glowing reviews, unlock new sets in the shop, and take on bigger homes all over town. Daily and weekly goals give you something sweet to aim for.

☕

Cozy, at your pace

No timers and no rush. Play a quick job on the bus or spend a whole evening fussing over every corner. Your progress stays safely on your phone.
