Privacy policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Read the Room ("the game") is made by MICHAEL BROOKS ADMINISTRATION. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

What we collect

We do not collect anything ourselves. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics of our own, and we never see your name, email or saved game.

The game shows optional video ads through Google AdMob (see "Ads" below). When an ad loads, Google, not us, receives information from your device.

What stays on your device

Your saved game, settings (sound and music volume) and tutorial progress are stored only on your phone. They never leave your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

Internet access

The game uses the internet to load ads from Google AdMob. Everything else works offline.

Ads

Ads in the game are always optional. You choose to watch a short video in return for an in-game bonus, such as extra money for a finished job or a new client request. The game never shows an ad you did not ask for, and nothing in the game requires watching one.

The ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (you can reset it or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's Google settings),
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be worked out,
  • information about your device and the app, such as model, operating system, language and how you interact with the ads,
  • diagnostic and crash data from the ad SDK.

This information goes to Google and is handled under Google's privacy policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. We only receive anonymous totals from Google, such as how many ads were shown.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can say no and still play and watch ads, which are then not personalised. The game only requests family-friendly ads.

Purchases

The game does not currently offer any purchases. If a later version adds an optional purchase through Google Play, payment is handled entirely by Google Play, and this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
