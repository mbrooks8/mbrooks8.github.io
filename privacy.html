Privacy policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Read the Room ("the game") is made by MICHAEL BROOKS ADMINISTRATION. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, store or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your saved game, settings (sound and music volume) and tutorial progress are stored only on your phone. They never leave your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

Internet access

The game uses the internet permission only to load its own content. It does not send information about you anywhere.

Ads and purchases

The current version shows no ads and offers no purchases. If a later version adds optional rewarded ads (Google AdMob) or an in-app purchase (Google Play Billing), Google may process device information as described in Google's privacy policy, and this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
