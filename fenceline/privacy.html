Fenceline privacy

Last updated 28 November 2026. This covers the Fenceline app for Android and the web version at mbrooks8.github.io/fenceline.

Fenceline is made by Michael Brooks. The short version: there are no accounts and we never see your games. The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google receives some information from your phone to show them (see Ads below).

What stays on your device

Your settings, stats, puzzle history, saved game and timelapses are kept on your device only. Fenceline does not send them anywhere. Uninstalling the app (or clearing the site's data in your browser) removes them.

Playing together

Play together is the only part of Fenceline that talks to other devices, and only while you are using it.

  • Same room (Android): phones find each other directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi using Google Play services' Nearby Connections. No server is involved. Android asks for the Nearby devices permission (and Location on older Android versions) because that is how phones are found. Fenceline never reads or keeps your location.
  • Online: players connect directly to each other. To help them find each other, the game uses PeerJS, a free public connection service, which sees your device's internet address and a random room ID while you play. If a direct connection isn't possible, game moves may pass through a relay server. Fenceline doesn't use these services for anything else.
  • What other players see: the name you type (or your device's name if you leave it blank), your pencil colour, and your moves in that game. A random ID lets you get your seat back if you drop out.

Ads

The Android app from Google Play shows ads from Google AdMob: a small banner under the puzzle, now and then a short video between puzzles, and, if you choose, a short video for an extra hint. The web version, the app downloaded from this website and test versions have no ads. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google's ad software may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (you can reset it or turn off personalised ads in your phone's Google settings),
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be worked out,
  • information about your device and the app, such as model, system, language and how you interact with the ads,
  • diagnostic and crash data from the ad software.

This goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. We only get anonymous totals, such as how many ads were shown.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can say no and keep playing. Only family-friendly ads are requested.

Sharing

When you share a puzzle, the link holds only the puzzle's shape, size, difficulty and number. You choose where it goes.

What Fenceline never does

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics of our own.
  • We never sell your information. Apart from the ads above, nothing is shared with anyone.

Children

Fenceline is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.
